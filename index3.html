Golden Ratio — Fun Face Analyzer

A playful browser experience that detects facial landmarks, analyzes facial proportions using Golden Ratio-inspired measurements, and replies with funny, entertaining sentences based on the result.

  • Creative thinkingA serious-looking idea made deliberately lighthearted.
  • Facial landmark analysisKey points on the face are detected and turned into proportions.
  • Interactive web developmentBuilt to respond instantly to the person using it.
  • HumorResults arrive as playful one-liners, never as a verdict on anyone.

Just for fun. This project is made purely for entertainment and experimentation. It is not a scientific assessment of beauty, attractiveness or personal worth.
